SLIS Capstone Portfolio

by Jessica MF

Welcome to my Capstone Portfolio! As you explore this site, I hope you enjoy my work samples and reflections on what I've learned during my time at the University of Iowa's School of Library and Information Science. As you'll see, each page focuses on a different core competency of librarianship. Since I matriculated into the graduate program in August 2024, the artifacts in my portfolio align with the American Library Association's nine Core Competences of Librarianship, which inform the SLIS competencies.

Professional Statement

As a Teen Reference Librarian, I have come to understand that the role of an information specialist extends far beyond traditional librarian duties. This MLIS program has equipped me with knowledge in archival activism, community engagement, leadership skills, and more that informs my practice of librarianship. My professional philosophy is grounded in critical librarianship, restorative justice practices, and feminist pedagogy. I believe it is essential to advocate for equitable access to information and resources, particularly for marginalized groups, especially teens, children, and unhoused patrons. My experience in both education and library services has highlighted the need for collaboration and continual learning, leading me to question policies that may inadvertently exclude or punish vulnerable populations. Collaboration with colleagues has strengthened my resolve to foster an inclusive environment for staff and patrons.

In the next five years, I plan to continue my advocacy work and further develop my skills through workshops, trainings, and professional networks. I hope to publish in professional journals or websites and present at conferences. By remaining open to feedback and actively engaging with my community, I hope to grow both personally and professionally, staying true to my commitment to serve as an empowered advocate for all patrons.
