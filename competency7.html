Research and Evidence-Based Practice

My Research Poster assignment for SLIS 5600: Reading Culture: History and Research in Media aligns with ALA's Core Competence on Research and Evidence-Based Practice, as it involved discovering and generating research related to my work as a Teen Reference Librarian and former English Teacher. I focused on how digital tools impact literacy for underserved teens like those who visit my library branch.

With limited library research on this topic, I took a multidisciplinary approach, exploring library and education databases and school achievement data to "discover, engage with, and synthesize existing research" for my literature review (7A). I designed a survey for local students to generate data, analyzed it alongside existing research, and compiled and presented my conclusions (7B). The project progressed through an abstract, initial bibliography, and peer feedback, and culminated in a virtual poster session.

This assignment emphasized the importance of research and the role of scholarly communications in fostering ongoing professional growth, knowledge, and sharing (7D). I shared my findings with classmates and with colleagues who plan programming for the same teens involved in the study. As a result, we became more aware of these teens' literacy levels and how they use digital tools in their daily lives. This awareness allows us to incorporate literacy more intentionally and in engaging ways.
