3. Lifelong Learning and Continuing Education

My research paper and presentation for SLIS 6145: Digital Preservation and Stewardship demonstrate alignment with ALA's Core Competence on Lifelong Learning and Continuing Education. The topics my classmates and I selected align with the iPRES 2026 conference theme: "Community, Preservation, and Horizon." We structured our work like professionals, starting with a proposal and peer-review process and culminating in a mini online conference where we presented our research, followed by Q&A sessions.

I chose the topic, "Preserving Culture, Preventing Harm: Ethical Risks of Social Media Preservation for Immigrant Communities," as I work with many Spanish-speaking immigrants at my public library. They rely on social media apps like Facebook and WhatsApp for information about community events and resources. I was curious how preserving those records could impact them, especially given increasing concerns about ICE raids in our community at the time (3A).

I learned that libraries play a role in continuing education and lifelong learning initiatives not only for patrons and community members, but also for information professionals within institutions and across the wider field (3B). As an educator and librarian, I've attended many professional development days and conferences and presented at smaller events. This project enhanced my confidence in conducting and presenting in-depth research on complex topics. I actively engage in on-going professional development and plan to lead initiatives and present at conferences in the future. I hope to inspire my colleagues and contribute to the growth of our field.

Professional Development

Attending the national REFORMA conference in Denver, Colorado, in September 2027 will allow me to learn from library professionals working to improve library and information services for Latine and Spanish-speaking communities. I have already spoken with my manager about attending, as the conference will give me the opportunity to identify new approaches, build professional connections, and bring relevant ideas and resources back to my library branch and our large Latine and Spanish-speaking patron base. This experience will strengthen my commitment to ongoing professional development and help me better serve my community.
